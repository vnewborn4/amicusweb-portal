Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 • Published by Amicus Technology Services Inc. (Texas Registered C-Corp)

At Amicus Technology Services Inc. ("Amicus," "we," "our," or "us"), we are committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and proprietary integrity of our customers and website visitors. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and safeguard information across the Amicus Web Assistant™ platform, amicusweb.dev, amicus-technology.net, and the underlying WebDaaS™ infrastructure.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to deliver enterprise-grade autonomous web engineering services:

  • Account & Corporate Identity Data: Name, corporate email address, organization name, commercial telephone number, and authentication tokens.
  • Billing & Payment Information: Payment transactions processed securely via PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant providers (Stripe, Link, Apple Pay, Google Pay). We never store raw credit card numbers or banking secrets on our servers.
  • System Telemetry & Audit Logs: IP addresses, browser types, operating systems, and audit log telemetry generated during synthesis operations for security monitoring and DDoS mitigation.
  • User Prompts & Directives: Code snippets, layout requirements, and project specifications submitted to the WebDaaS™ synthesizer.

2. Strict Zero-Retention AI Training Guarantee

We value proprietary client data. Client code, project architecture, prompts, and synthesized components are NEVER used to train public or foundational third-party AI models. All interactions with underlying inference endpoints adhere to enterprise zero-data-retention standards with complete isolation.

3. 100% Commercial Code Ownership

You own 100% of the code, components, and assets generated by Amicus Web Assistant™. We claim zero rights, licensing encumbrances, or royalties over your synthesized deliverables.

4. Security, Transport & Storage Controls

All platform communications are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 Strict Transport Security and encrypted at rest using AES-256 GCM. Code changes, deployments, and systemic operations are timestamped with cryptographic SHA-256 hashes to guarantee provenance and audit integrity.

5. Compliance with CCPA, GDPR and Texas Privacy Laws

Under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA), CCPA, and GDPR, users have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of their personal data. Requests can be submitted directly to our Data Protection Officer at admin@amicus-technology.net.
