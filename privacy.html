Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026 • Published by Amicus Technology Services Inc.

At Amicus Technology Services Inc. ("Amicus," "we," "our," or "us"), we are committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and proprietary integrity of our customers and visitors. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and safeguard information across the Amicus Web Assistant™ platform and WebDaaS™ infrastructure.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to deliver enterprise-grade autonomous web development services:

  • Account & Identity Data: Name, corporate email address, organization name, commercial phone number, and account credentials.
  • Billing & Payment Information: Payment transactions processed securely via PCI-DSS compliant providers (Stripe, Link, Apple Pay, Google Pay). We never store raw credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: IP addresses, browser types, operating systems, and audit log telemetry generated during synthesis operations.
  • User-Generated Prompts & Architecture Directives: Code snippets, layout requirements, and project specifications submitted to the WebDaaS™ synthesizer.

2. Zero-Retention AI Training Guarantee

We value proprietary client data. Client code, project architecture, prompts, and synthesized components are NEVER used to train public or foundational third-party AI models. All interactions with underlying inference endpoints adhere to enterprise zero-data-retention standards.

3. Security & Cryptographic Protection

All platform communications are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and encrypted at rest using AES-256. Code changes, deployments, and systemic operations are timestamped with cryptographic SHA-256 hashes to guarantee provenance and audit integrity.

4. Compliance with CCPA and GDPR

Users have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of their personal data. Requests can be submitted directly to our Data Protection Officer at admin@amicus-technology.net.
